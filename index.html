Privacy Policy for Zack

Last Updated: September 2026

1. 100% Local Processing

Zack is designed from the ground up as a local-first, offline application. All artificial intelligence processing, inference, and data generation occur entirely on your physical device's hardware.

2. Zero Cloud Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store, or monitor your prompts, conversations, or personal data. Your interactions with Zack never leave your phone, meaning there are no external servers, no cloud backups on our end, and no data harvesting.

3. Third-Party Analytics

Zack does not include third-party advertising trackers or invasive analytics SDKs. The app only accesses the necessary local device permissions required to function.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at: support@ask-zack.com
